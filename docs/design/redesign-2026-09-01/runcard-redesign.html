Analyses cpptraj

all structure dynamics ensemble
Backbone RMSDstructure
How far the protein backbone has drifted from its starting shape. Climbing early then flattening is normal settling.
Per-residue RMSFdynamics
Which residues wiggle the most. Peaks mark flexible loops or termini, not necessarily a problem.
Radius of gyrationstructure
How compact vs. extended the structure is over time. A stable band suggests the fold isn't unraveling.
SASAstructure
Surface area exposed to solvent. Sudden jumps can mean partial unfolding or a pocket opening up.
DSSPdynamics
Secondary structure per residue over time — which parts are helix, sheet, or coil, and when that changes.
H-bondsdynamics
Protein–ligand hydrogen bonds present per frame. Frequent flips are normal for a weak or transient contact.
Cα–Cα distance matrixensemble
Pairwise distances between backbone atoms — a fingerprint of the overall shape.
Clusteringensemble
Groups frames into recurring shapes. A few dominant clusters mean the trajectory settled somewhere consistent.
PCA (Cα)ensemble
The trajectory's motion projected onto its two biggest directions of variance — nearby dots are structurally similar.
